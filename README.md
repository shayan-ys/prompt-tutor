# prompt-tutor

Reviews the English of the prompts you type into your coding agent, without interrupting you and without adding anything to the agent's context. After each prompt it reports spelling, grammar, and fluency errors, gives a native-speaker rewrite and one tip, and once a week it writes a digest of your recurring error patterns.

**Status: planning.** Nothing is built yet. The design is being charted in the [wayfinder map](https://github.com/shayan-ys/prompt-tutor/issues?q=label%3Awayfinder%3Amap). Vocabulary is in [GLOSSARY.md](./GLOSSARY.md); decisions are in [docs/adr/](./docs/adr/).
